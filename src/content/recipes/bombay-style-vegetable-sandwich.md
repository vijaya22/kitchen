## Ingredients

- 4 slices bread
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- 1/2 medium cucumber, thinly sliced
- 2 cheese slices
- 1–2 tbsp softened butter
- 1/2 tsp chaat masala, or to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- Salt, only if needed
- 2 tbsp thick green chutney (optional)
- 1 small boiled potato, cooled and thinly sliced (optional)

## Method

1. **Prepare the vegetables.** Slice the tomato and cucumber. Pat away excess moisture with a clean kitchen towel so the bread doesn't turn soggy. If using boiled potato, peel and slice it thinly.

2. **Layer the sandwich.** Spread a thin layer of butter on one side of each bread slice, then add green chutney over the butter, if using. Place a cheese slice on two of the bread slices and layer with cucumber, tomato, and the potato, if using. Sprinkle lightly with chaat masala and black pepper. Add salt only if needed, as the cheese and chaat masala already contain salt.

3. **Toast until golden.** Close the sandwiches with the remaining bread and lightly butter the outsides. Place on a preheated tawa or frying pan over medium-low heat. Toast for 2–3 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until the bread is golden and crisp and the cheese begins to soften.

4. **Slice and serve.** Cut into halves and serve immediately, with green chutney or tomato ketchup on the side.

---

**Tips:** Pat the cucumber and tomato slices dry before assembling to keep the filling from making the bread soggy. Use a thick chutney — a watery one can quickly soak through the bread. Toast over gentle heat so the cheese has time to soften without burning the bread. Spread the vegetables evenly so each bite has a little of everything. Serve it freshly toasted — the contrast between crisp bread and juicy vegetables is best straight from the pan.
