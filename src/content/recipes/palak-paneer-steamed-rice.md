## Ingredients

**For the palak paneer**

- 250 g paneer, cut into cubes
- 300 g spinach, washed thoroughly and thick stems removed
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 small tomato, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, or to taste
- 1 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- Salt, to taste
- 1/4–1/2 cup water, as needed
- 1–2 tbsp fresh cream (optional)

**For the rice**

- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1 1/2 cups water, or as per the rice's package instructions
- A pinch of salt (optional)

## Method

1. **Cook the rice.** Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Soak for 15–20 minutes, then drain. Add it to a saucepan with the measured water and a pinch of salt, if using. Bring to a boil, cover, and reduce the heat to low. Cook for 10–12 minutes until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender. Switch off the heat and leave it covered for 5–10 minutes before gently fluffing with a fork.

2. **Blanch the spinach.** Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add the spinach and blanch for 1–2 minutes, just until wilted. Drain and transfer to cold water to stop the cooking.

3. **Make the purée.** Drain the spinach again and blend it with the green chilli into a smooth purée, adding only a little water if needed.

4. **Temper the cumin.** Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle.

5. **Cook the onions.** Add the onion and cook until lightly golden. Stir in the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute.

6. **Build the gravy base.** Add the tomato, coriander powder, turmeric, and salt. Cook until the tomato softens and the mixture comes together.

7. **Add the spinach.** Stir in the spinach purée and add enough water to make a thick but spoonable gravy. Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes.

8. **Add the paneer.** Add the paneer and garam masala. Stir gently and cook on low heat for another 2–3 minutes, just until the paneer is heated through.

9. **Serve warm.** Taste and adjust the seasoning. Serve warm with the steamed rice, spooning a little spinach gravy over the rice as you eat.

---

**Tips:** For a richer gravy, stir in 1–2 tbsp of fresh cream at the end over low heat — I skipped it in mine, but you can add it for a creamier texture. If the paneer feels firm, soak it in warm water for 10 minutes and drain before adding. Add water gradually, since the spinach already holds moisture and the gravy can turn watery. Keep the cooking gentle — cooking it too long dulls the spinach's colour and makes the paneer firm.
