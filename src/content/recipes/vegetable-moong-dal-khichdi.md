## Ingredients

*Quantities below are suggested proportions.*

**For the khichdi**

- 1/2 cup rice
- 1/2 cup yellow moong dal
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt, to taste
- 4 cups water, plus hot water as needed

**For the vegetable tadka**

- 1–1 1/2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
- 1–2 green chillies, slit
- 1 small carrot, diced
- 1/2 cup green peas
- 1/4 cup chopped green beans
- 2–4 tbsp water, as needed
- 1 tbsp chopped coriander (optional, for finishing)

## Method

1. **Prepare the rice and dal.** Rinse the rice and moong dal thoroughly. If you have time, soak them together for 15–20 minutes, then drain.

2. **Cook the khichdi base.** Add the rice, dal, turmeric, salt, and 4 cups of water to a pressure cooker. Pressure-cook until the rice and dal are very soft, following your cooker's instructions, and let the pressure release naturally before opening. Stir well to bring the rice and dal together into a soft, creamy mixture.

3. **Make the vegetable tadka.** Heat ghee in a separate pan over medium heat. Add the jeera and let it sizzle, then add the green chillies. Add the carrot, green beans, and peas and sauté for 2–3 minutes. Add a splash of water, cover, and cook over medium-low heat until the vegetables are tender, stirring occasionally.

4. **Combine and simmer.** Add the vegetable tadka to the cooked rice and dal. Stir well, adding hot water a little at a time to reach your preferred consistency. Simmer gently for 3–5 minutes so the flavours come together. Taste and adjust the salt.

5. **Finish and serve.** Finish with chopped coriander, if using. Serve hot on its own or with curd, pickle, or papad alongside.

---

**Tips:** Cook the rice and dal until they blend together easily when stirred. Cut the vegetables small so they cook evenly and mix nicely through the khichdi. Adjust the consistency at the end — khichdi thickens as it rests, so keep a little hot water ready. Use a slit green chilli for a gentler flavour, or chop it for more heat. To reheat, add a splash of water and stir over low heat until hot and creamy again.
