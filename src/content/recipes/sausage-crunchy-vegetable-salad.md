## Ingredients

**For the salad**

- 3 cups lettuce, roughly torn
- 1 medium cucumber, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 small carrot, peeled and cut into thin strips
- 1/4 cup black olives, sliced
- 2–3 fully cooked sausages, sliced into rounds
- 1 tsp olive oil, for the pan

**For the lime vinaigrette**

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- Salt, to taste
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 tsp honey (optional, to balance the tang)

## Method

1. **Prepare the vegetables.** Wash and thoroughly dry the lettuce. Chop the cucumber, cut the carrot into thin strips, and slice the olives. Place everything in a large mixing bowl.

2. **Brown the sausages.** Heat a teaspoon of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the sliced sausages and cook for 5–8 minutes, turning occasionally, until lightly browned and heated through. Set aside to cool slightly so they don't wilt the lettuce.

3. **Make the lime vinaigrette.** Whisk together the olive oil, lime juice, salt, and black pepper. Add the honey if you prefer a gentler tang. Taste and adjust the lime juice or seasoning as needed.

4. **Toss and serve.** Pour the dressing over the vegetables a little at a time and toss gently until lightly coated. Top with the warm sausage slices and serve immediately.

---

**Tips:** Dry the lettuce well so the dressing clings to the leaves. Slice the carrot thinly — fine strips make it easier to get a little of everything in each bite. Dress the salad just before serving to keep the lettuce and cucumber crisp. Go easy on the salt, since the olives and sausages already add saltiness. If you're using raw sausages, cook them fully according to the package instructions before slicing. To make it ahead, keep the vegetables, sausages, and dressing separate until ready to serve.
