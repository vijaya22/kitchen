## Ingredients

*Quantities are suggested proportions.*

- 4–6 chicken kebabs, prepared using my [Air-Fried Chicken Kebabs recipe](/recipes/air-fried-chicken-kebabs/)
- 1 cup boiled peas, drained and cooled
- 1 medium cucumber, chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 cup finely chopped kale, tough stems removed
- 1/4 cup ricotta cheese
- 1–2 tbsp fresh lemon juice, to taste
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. **Prepare the chicken kebabs.** Make the kebabs using my [Air-Fried Chicken Kebabs recipe](/recipes/air-fried-chicken-kebabs/). Set aside to cool slightly while you prepare the salad.

2. **Prepare the vegetables.** Wash and dry the cucumber, tomato, and kale. Chop the cucumber and tomato into bite-sized pieces and finely chop the kale. Make sure the boiled peas are well drained so they don't make the salad watery.

3. **Soften the kale.** Place the kale in a mixing bowl with a little of the lemon juice and a small pinch of salt. Gently massage for 30–60 seconds to soften the leaves.

4. **Assemble the salad.** Add the peas, cucumber, and tomato to the kale. Pour over the remaining lemon juice, add salt to taste, and toss gently.

5. **Add the ricotta and kebabs.** Spoon small dollops of ricotta over the vegetables. Arrange the chicken kebabs alongside or on top. Serve immediately, with an extra squeeze of lemon if you like.

---

**Tips:** Keep the dressing light — lemon juice and salt are enough, and the ricotta adds creaminess. Drain the peas well and let them cool before adding them to keep the vegetables crisp. Chop the kale finely, remove the tough stems, and massage the leaves briefly for a softer bite. Add the ricotta last, spooning it gently over the salad so it stays in soft pockets. To make it ahead, keep the cooked kebabs, vegetables, ricotta, and lemon dressing separate until serving.
