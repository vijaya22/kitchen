## Ingredients

*Quantities below are suggested proportions.*

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1–2 tbsp cream cheese
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp butter or oil, for frying
- Salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. **Make the avocado spread.** Scoop the avocado into a bowl. Add the cream cheese and a little salt, then mash with a fork until combined. Leave it slightly chunky or mash until smooth, whichever you prefer.

2. **Toast the sourdough.** Toast the sourdough in a toaster or on a hot pan until golden and crisp at the edges.

3. **Fry the eggs.** Heat butter or oil in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Crack in the eggs and cook gently, without flipping, until the whites are fully set and the yolks reach your preferred consistency. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.

4. **Assemble and serve.** Spread the avocado mixture generously over the warm sourdough. Place a fried egg on each slice and serve immediately.

---

**Tips:** Use a ripe avocado — it should yield gently when pressed and mash easily. Let the cream cheese sit out briefly so it mixes smoothly into the avocado. Salt lightly, since cream cheese already contains salt, and taste the spread before adding more. Cook the eggs gently; lower heat helps the whites set without making the underside tough. Assemble just before eating to keep the sourdough crisp and the avocado fresh.
