## Ingredients

*Quantities below are suggested proportions.*

- 2 slices whole wheat bread, or bread of your choice
- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup boiled sweet corn, drained
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp grated cheddar or processed cheese
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp butter (optional, for toasting)

## Method

1. **Boil the eggs.** Place the eggs in a saucepan and cover with water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer and cook for 10–12 minutes, until hard-boiled. Transfer to cold water. Once cooled, peel and chop into small pieces.

2. **Make the topping.** Add the chopped eggs, cooled sweet corn, mayonnaise, and grated cheese to a bowl. Sprinkle in the black pepper and a little salt. Mix gently until everything is coated, keeping some texture in the eggs.

3. **Toast the bread.** Toast the bread until golden and crisp. If using a pan, spread a thin layer of butter on the bread and toast both sides over medium-low heat.

4. **Assemble and serve.** Spoon the egg, cheese, and corn mixture generously over each slice. Spread it evenly to the edges and serve immediately.

---

**Tips:** Let the eggs and corn cool before adding the mayonnaise. Drain the corn well, as excess water can make the topping loose and the toast soggy. Salt lightly — mayonnaise and cheese already contain salt, so taste before adding more. Fold gently so the topping keeps little chunks of egg. If preparing ahead, keep the topping refrigerated separately and spoon it onto freshly toasted bread just before serving.
