## Ingredients

**For the cake**

- 1 packet [Betty Crocker Chocolate Fudge Cake Mix](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B00LJWF08A?tag=kitchenvijaya-21)
- 250 ml water
- 3 medium eggs
- 100 ml vegetable oil (the packet quantity — see the tips for my butter variation)
- 1/2–1 tsp [vanilla essence](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0BFX79VRQ?tag=kitchenvijaya-21)
- 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup chopped chocolate

**For decorating**

- Nutella, enough to cover the top
- Whipped cream, enough for a heart and decorative border

*Vanilla, cocoa, and chocolate quantities are suggested amounts.*

## Method

1. **Prepare the pans.** For the packet's oven method, preheat to 180°C and grease two 23 cm / 9-inch round pans. Line their bases with baking paper. If using a convection microwave, use convection mode and follow the appliance's guidance for suitable bakeware and preheating — these temperature and timing instructions are not for microwave-only mode.

2. **Mix the batter.** Combine the cake mix, water, eggs, and oil in a large bowl. Add the vanilla essence and sift in the extra cocoa powder. Mix on low speed until moistened, then beat on medium speed for 2 minutes, or beat vigorously by hand for 3 minutes. Gently fold in the chopped chocolate.

3. **Bake.** Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans. The packet recommends 22–27 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out without wet batter. Test away from a chocolate piece, as melted chocolate can leave a smear. If using a different pan size or one deeper pan, the baking time will change.

4. **Cool completely.** Leave the cakes in their pans for 10 minutes, then carefully turn them out onto a wire rack. Cool completely before decorating.

5. **Add the Nutella.** Place the cooled cake on a serving plate. Spread Nutella over the top with a spoon or spatula.

6. **Decorate.** Put the whipped cream in a piping bag. Pipe and fill a heart in the centre, then add curved lines or swirls around the edge. Keep the decorated cake refrigerated until serving.

---

**Tips:** I used both oil and butter. The packet specifies 100 ml oil, so if you're recreating my variation, replace part of that oil with melted, cooled butter rather than adding butter on top of the full oil quantity. Keep the extra cocoa modest — too much can make the cake dry or bitter. Cool the cake completely before decorating, as warm cake will soften the Nutella and melt the whipped cream. Room-temperature Nutella spreads more easily without lifting crumbs. Keep the cream cold and whip until it holds its shape for easier piping. Check the pan size — the packet's 22–27-minute timing is for batter divided between two 9-inch pans.
