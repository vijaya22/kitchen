## Ingredients

*Serves 2 as a side.*

- 2 packed cups arugula (rocket), washed and dried
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved lengthwise
- 100 g bocconcini, drained and torn in half
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- A pinch of salt
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp yellow mustard (I used French's Classic Yellow Mustard)
- 1/2 tsp honey, optional
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano
- A pinch of chilli flakes

## Method

1. **Toss the base.** Put the arugula and cherry tomatoes in a bowl. Drizzle with the olive oil, add a pinch of salt and toss gently.

2. **Make the dressing.** Whisk together the rice vinegar, lemon juice, yellow mustard, black pepper, oregano and chilli flakes in a small bowl. Taste and stir in the honey if you'd like to soften the tang.

3. **Dress and serve.** Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently to coat the leaves. Scatter the torn bocconcini over the top and serve immediately.

---

**Tips:** Pat the arugula dry so the dressing clings to the leaves. If you're using seasoned rice vinegar, taste the dressing before adding honey.
