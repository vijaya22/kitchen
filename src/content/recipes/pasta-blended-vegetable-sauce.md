## Ingredients

*Quantities below are suggested proportions.*

- 200 g dried pasta
- 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 medium bell pepper, chopped
- 1 small carrot, thinly sliced
- 3–4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- Salt, to taste
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper, or to taste
- 1/4 cup grated cheese
- 1/2–1 cup reserved pasta cooking water, as needed
- 1–2 tbsp grated Parmesan, for serving

## Method

1. **Sauté the vegetables.** Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened. Add the carrot and bell pepper and sauté for 5–7 minutes, then add the tomatoes, salt, and black pepper. Cook until all the vegetables are tender and the tomatoes have softened. Add a splash of water if the vegetables begin to stick.

2. **Cool and blend.** Take the pan off the heat and let the vegetables cool. Blend into a smooth paste, adding a little water only if needed to help the blender along.

3. **Cook the pasta.** Meanwhile, boil the pasta in salted water according to the package instructions, until al dente. Reserve a cup of the cooking water before draining.

4. **Make the sauce.** Heat the remaining tablespoon of olive oil in a separate pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and sauté for 30–60 seconds, until fragrant but not browned. Add the blended vegetable paste and cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Loosen with a little reserved pasta water until it reaches a sauce-like consistency.

5. **Add the cheese and pasta.** Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the grated cheese until melted, then add the cooked pasta. Toss gently until the pasta is evenly coated. Add another splash of pasta water if needed, and taste before adjusting the salt and black pepper.

6. **Garnish and serve.** Divide between bowls and finish with grated Parmesan. Serve warm.

---

**Tips:** Cook the carrot until tender — it blends more smoothly and gives the sauce a natural sweetness. Let the sautéed vegetables cool before transferring them to a closed blender jar. Save the pasta water and add it gradually to loosen the sauce and help it coat the pasta. Keep the heat low when adding the cheese and stir gently so it melts into the sauce. Taste before adding extra salt, since both the cheese and Parmesan contribute saltiness.
