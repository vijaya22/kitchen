## Ingredients

- 1 medium beetroot
- 1 cup plain curd
- Salt, to taste

**For the tadka**

- 1 tsp oil
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
- 6–8 curry leaves

## Method

1. **Boil the beetroot.** Wash the beetroot and boil until tender — a knife should slide through easily. This takes around 25–35 minutes, depending on its size. Let it cool, then peel and grate.

2. **Mix with curd.** Whisk the curd until smooth. Add the cooled, grated beetroot and salt, then mix well. The curd will turn a lovely pink as the beetroot blends into it.

3. **Make the tadka.** Heat oil in a small tadka pan. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle briefly. Add the curry leaves carefully, as they may splutter, and cook for a few seconds until fragrant.

4. **Finish and serve.** Pour the tadka over the beetroot-curd mixture. Serve with the tadka on top, or stir it through just before eating. Enjoy as a side with dal-rice, khichdi, or a simple roti-sabzi meal.

---

**Tips:** Let the beetroot cool completely before mixing it with the curd. For a thicker salad, use thick curd and drain any excess water from the boiled beetroot before grating. Pat the curry leaves dry before adding them to the hot oil to reduce spluttering. To make it ahead, refrigerate the beetroot-curd mixture and add the fresh tadka just before serving.
