## Ingredients

*Prep time assumes the pulses are already cooked.*

**For the salad**

- 3/4 cup cooked kala chana, drained and cooled
- 3/4 cup cooked whole green moong, drained and cooled
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped

**For the dressing**

- **2–3 drops mustard oil**
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, or to taste
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. **Prepare the pulses.** If starting with dried pulses, soak the kala chana and whole green moong separately, then drain and cook in fresh water until tender. Keep the moong intact rather than cooking it until mushy. Drain well and let both cool completely.

2. **Chop the vegetables.** Chop the onion and tomato into small, bite-sized pieces.

3. **Combine the salad.** Place the kala chana, green moong, onion, and tomato in a bowl and toss gently to combine.

4. **Add the dressing.** Add 2–3 drops of mustard oil, the lemon juice, and salt. Mix thoroughly so the dressing coats the salad evenly. Taste and adjust the lemon juice and salt as needed.

5. **Serve.** Serve freshly mixed as a snack or alongside a meal.

---

**Tips:** A few drops of mustard oil are enough — keep it to 2–3 drops for the whole salad, as its flavour is strong. Cook the pulses separately, since kala chana takes longer to soften than green moong. Drain them thoroughly, as excess cooking water will dilute the lemon dressing. The moong should be tender but still hold its shape. To make it ahead, refrigerate the cooked pulses and add the chopped vegetables and dressing shortly before serving.
