## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups whole wheat flour (about 180 g)
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar (about 60 g), adjusted to the sweetness of the bananas
- 3 eggs
- 4 medium ripe bananas, mashed
- 1 tsp vanilla essence
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips
- 2–3 tbsp shaved almonds, for the topping

## Method

1. **Prepare the oven and tin.** Preheat the oven to 175°C conventional / 160°C fan. Line a 9-inch round cake tin with baking paper.

2. **Mix the wet ingredients.** Beat the eggs and powdered sugar in a large bowl until slightly pale and frothy. Add the mashed bananas and vanilla essence, and mix until combined.

3. **Add the dry ingredients.** In a separate bowl, whisk together the whole wheat flour and baking soda. Add this to the banana mixture and fold gently until no dry flour remains. Avoid overmixing so the cake stays tender.

4. **Add the chocolate chips.** Fold the chocolate chips into the batter. Pour into the prepared tin and gently level the top. Scatter the shaved almonds evenly over the surface.

5. **Bake.** Bake for 40–50 minutes, checking from around 35 minutes. The cake is ready when a skewer inserted into the centre comes out without wet batter — a little melted chocolate is fine. If the almonds brown before the centre is cooked, loosely cover the top with foil and continue baking.

6. **Cool and slice.** Let the cake rest in the tin for 10–15 minutes, then carefully transfer it to a wire rack. Let it cool before slicing so the banana-rich crumb can set.

---

**Tips:** Use very ripe bananas — ones with brown spots mash easily and bring more sweetness. If your bananas are very sweet and you're using milk chocolate chips, you can reduce the sugar slightly. Fold gently, since whole wheat batter can turn dense if mixed too much after adding the flour. With four bananas and no added butter or oil, expect a moist, hearty crumb that's more substantial than a light sponge. Check the centre before taking it out — banana cake can look browned on top before it has finished baking inside.
